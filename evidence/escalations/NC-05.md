# NC-05 — The ladder for client managers and employees — Evidence

- Date: 2026-10-11
- Status: **done**
- Decision: ADR-021 (rev. 2 records six points settled here). Card approved in advance ("approved for all").

## What changed

**Engine.** One set of rules now serves three audiences. `reach()` works out which obligations have reached which stage; three builders address the result:

| Builder | Shows | Owner shown | Told | Mine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `buildLadder` (staff) | everything the viewer may read | name or role | by stage and role | if they own it |
| `buildClientLadder` | only stages that name "the client": a lapsed document, a request past its service level | never | always | never |
| `buildEmployeeLadder` | every stage of their own documents, their dependants' and their own requests | never | always | always |

**What each audience's ladder is built from** (the service reads only this):

| | Client manager | Employee |
|---|---|---|
| People | their company's, and **only while their portal is on** | their own record, already fetched and checked by the `/me` gate |
| Dependants | none (ADR-017 gives clients no view) | their own, through the employee's fenced read |
| Requests | their company's, through the client's fenced read; not tied to the portal | the ones they raised, through the employee's fenced read |
| Procedures | their company's, used only to keep a document quiet | their own, on the staff connection, used only to keep a document quiet |
| Tasks | none | none |

**Routes.**

| Route | Who |
|---|---|
| `GET /escalations`, `GET /escalations/count`, `POST /escalations/read` | staff and, now, client managers (`escalation.read`). `scopeOf` picks the path from the session and refuses anyone else. |
| `GET /me/escalations`, `GET /me/escalations/count`, `POST /me/escalations/read` | employees (`self-service.read`), behind the same gate as the rest of `/me` |

Read marks work the same for all three: only what the caller is shown can be marked, and marks are per person.

**Screen.** The panel asks for the right ladder for whoever is signed in:

| | Tabs | Line under each item | Opens | Footer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Client manager | Needs action · Updates | "PEOPLE&GRO team · document expiry" | their register, or the request | how many lapsed documents the team is already renewing; Escalation rules (read-only) |
| Employee | Needs action · About me · Updates | "document expiry" | their file, or their request | "The PEOPLE&GRO team is notified on the same schedule, and acts before anything lapses." |

If an employee's company has self-service off, the ladder is refused and the panel falls back to the Updates list.

## Tests

- Engine: 7 more in `escalation-ladder.e2e-spec.ts` (24 in all).
- API: `escalation-audiences.e2e-spec.ts` (11), on the real database, signed in as two client managers of one company, a manager of another, and two employees of one company.
- The isolation harness now probes `GET /me/escalations` with a same-company colleague (3 more).
- Two earlier specs changed, because a client manager is no longer refused: they now assert what the manager gets instead, and the "holding the permission is not enough" proof moved from the client manager to the employee.

These tests were written **after** the code. Each rule was then broken to see a test fail:

| # | Broken | Failed as expected |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A client is told at every stage | yes |
| 2 | A client sees dependants | yes |
| 3 | A client's quiet count covers every stage | yes |
| 4 | An employee sees procedures | yes |
| 5 | The route treats a client as staff | yes (they would have seen the staff view) |
| 6 | A client's people are read with the portal off | yes |
| 7 | A client's requests come from every company | yes |
| 8 | A client's people come from every company | **no** — see below |
| 8b | The same, with the engine also handed every company | yes |
| 9 | An employee's requests are the whole company's | yes |
| 10 | An employee's renewal does not quiet the expiry | yes |
| 11 | The client manager loses the permission | yes (and the role matrix) |
| 12 | The `/me` gate's company switch is skipped | yes |

**Proof 8 did not fail, and the reason is a second guard, not a weak test.** When the service was made to read every company's people for a client, the engine still dropped them: it only watches people whose company is among the rows it was given, and it was given the client's own company alone. So two things keep another company's people out. Breaking both (8b) lets them through and the tests fail. I have left both in place.

- API suite: **847/847** (826 + 21). Lint and typecheck clean. `next build` passes.

## Stated plainly

- **A client manager with the portal off sees no person on the ladder**, only their requests. That matches what the portal itself shows them. On UAT the portal is off for the sample company unless an Administrator switches it on in Settings.
- **A lapsed document opens the client's register**, not that person. The client portal has no page for one person.
- **For a client the quiet line is worded for them** ("already being renewed by the PEOPLE&GRO team"). I changed that after seeing "in the work queue" on their screen: they have no work queue. The wording change was not re-checked in the browser; the build passes and the key exists in both languages.
- **An employee's procedures are read outside their own fence**, by the service, for one yes-or-no question per document. ADR-021 rev. 2 records it.
- **Self-Service now imports Escalations.** ADR-021 had said nothing imports it. Rev. 2 records the change and why.
