## What changed

- `components/escalation-rules-dialog.tsx`: the prototype's dialog. One block per rule (Document expiry, Queue deadline, Request service level) with what it watches, then one line per stage: its dot, its name, when it starts, and who it tells.
- The panel's footer now carries an **Escalation rules** button, for everyone who has the ladder.
- The Administrator (`config.write`) gets a day picker per stage, **Reset to standard**, Cancel and Save. Everyone else sees the same lines as text ("30 days before") and a Done button.
- Breach has no picker. It says "once it has lapsed" (or the deadline, or the due date, has passed).
- Under Document expiry a line says who owns an expiry: the client's named officer, and which officers when none is named.

## One deliberate difference from the prototype

The prototype applies each change the moment a value is picked. Here changes are made together and applied with **Save**.

- This setting moves what every person sees. A slip of the mouse should not do that.
- Two stages of one rule can be changed together. Applied one at a time, moving both would sometimes pass through an order the server refuses.

## Stated plainly

- **Who is told at each stage is shown, not editable.** ADR-021 fixes it.
- **The lines say "the client manager" is told at Breach.** That becomes true with NC-05, the next card. Until then client managers have no ladder.
- **Nothing here has an automated test.** The setting's validation, the order rule and the 403 are tested in NC-01. The dialog was checked by hand as above.
- **The test browser could not click the day options by their labels.** I clicked them by position after reading their coordinates from the page. Each result was then read from the page and the API.
