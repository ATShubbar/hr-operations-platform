## What was seen

The CI step "Seed the test database" failed about 3 s in, on some runs, and passed on a re-run.

## What was changed (at the time, unverified)

CI waits for the object store (MinIO) to be READY for five seconds running before seeding — the
image's entrypoint restarts the server after configuring it, and the old wait took its first sign
of life. A failed seed also prints the object store's log.
