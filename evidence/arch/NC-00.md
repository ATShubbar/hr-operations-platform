## What was compared

| | The prototype's panel | The app before this card |
|---|---|---|
| What it lists | An escalation ladder derived from the register: document expiry, queue deadlines, request service levels | Stored events: request decided, comment added, task or request assigned, leave decided |
| Stages | Notice, Reminder, Escalated, Breach; only the highest reached is shown | none |
| Who sees an item | Widens by stage: owner → HR → Administrator → client at Breach | the one person the event is about |
| Thresholds | Editable by the Administrator ("Escalation rules") | fixed |
| Read from | `design/` prototype: `NOTIF_STAGES`, `NOTIF_RULES`, `notifAll()`, `notifMine()` and the panel markup | `modules/notifications`, `components/notification-bell.tsx` |

Checked in the code, not assumed:

- `NotificationsModule` is imported by GRO, Self-Service and the queue modules, and imports no domain module. A ladder that reads people, procedures and requests therefore cannot live in it without an import loop. Hence a new delivery module.
- The daily expiry scan is gated by `flag.document-expiry-alerts`, which ships off.
- Marking a bell item read is already gated by `notification.read`; `escalation.read` follows that precedent for read marks.

## Decided in the ADR without asking, and why

- **Expiry dates come from the person's record and from dependants**, the same dates the runway and the People screen use, so the panel cannot disagree with them.
- **The Auditor is in no audience** and reads "Everything". The prototype has no Auditor.
- **Nobody is shown an item they could not already open.** A stage's audience never widens access.
- **A read mark carries the stage and the date**, so an item that climbs a stage or is renewed and comes round again is unread.
- **Within a rule a later stage may not start earlier than the one before it.** The prototype does not check this.
- **The named officer gains its first behaviour.** ADR-019 said the service facts carry none; ADR-021 changes that for the officer only and says so.

## Stated plainly

- **Two sets of expiry thresholds will exist** until stage emails are built: the old scan's (60/30/14/7/1/0, off by default) and the ladder's (30/14/7, editable). The ADR records it and binds the stage-email card to remove the overlap.
- **Insurance and driving licence on the employee are not in the ladder**, because they are still not stored.
- **An unassigned procedure or task is not shown at Notice stage.** There is nobody to tell until the stage that names a role.
- The ladder is computed on each load. I have not measured its cost; NC-01 will, on the seed data.
