### What each figure counts (`client-figures.ts`)

| Figure | Counts |
|---|---|
| Headcount | the company's employees, **leavers excluded** |
| Saudi share | Saudis among them **by nationality** — not Nitaqat, which weights some people differently |
| Expiring 30d | dated documents (iqama and permit for non-Saudis, contract end, passport) 0–30 days out, as on People |
| Open items | the company's **active GRO processes**. **Tasks are not counted**, though the card said "plus open tasks": tasks are own-scoped, so the same company would show each viewer a different number |
| Requests waiting | open requests |
| Runway | each stored document type × Overdue / 0–7 / 8–14 / 15–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 days, plus tracked. **"Overdue" is added**: in the prototype an expired document counts as tracked and appears in no column at all. Insurance and licence rows read "Not stored yet — coming soon" |

## Bugs caught while verifying

1. "Open items" came out in Arabic as «مهام مفتوحة» ("open tasks"), but it counts GRO
   procedures. It is now «بنود مفتوحة».
2. An empty register said "No one on the register" twice (subtitle + empty state). The subtitle
   now shows only when someone is on it.
3. **The DS-05 landmine, nearly repeated:** the register's `<col>` widths were first written
   with `calc()`, which a `<col>` ignores. They are plain percentages now.

## Notes

- **View register shows one more row than the register.** Alpha has 12 on People against 11
  on the register: People still lists a terminated employee (Rashed Alyami), while the record
  leaves leavers out of headcount and the register.
- The Saudi-share bar is **one colour**. The prototype paints it green / amber / red by
  Nitaqat-like thresholds, but those depend on sector and size; colouring a nationality count
  by them would make a judgement this figure can't.
- **The list's figures are computed in the browser** from every employee, GRO process and
  request (4 requests per load). That is fine at seed size; a real deployment needs a
  server-side summary.
- The seed stores no passport or contract-end dates, so those runway rows read 0 (the known
  seed gap).
