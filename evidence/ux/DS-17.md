**Deviations from the prototype, kept on purpose:**
- **Overdue column on the runway.** The same choice as the Client record (DS-10): without it, an expired document would sit in "Tracked" and in no column.
- **Client rows open the Client record (DS-10)**, not People filtered to the client. This is the same choice Reports made.
- **Saudisation is by nationality.** A note under the table says so, and says that Open items means active procedures (the `figuresFor` rule).
- **The tiles link to the screens that hold the rows:** Expiring → People (sorted soonest first), Past due → Work queue, Requests → Requests, Headcount → Clients.
