## Bugs caught while verifying

1. **The SS-01 source scan caught my code** (all three first suite runs: 1 failure). The new
   `principals()` mapped `principalType === 'client_rep' ? 'client' : …`, exactly the inline
   shape SS-01 forbids, because that is how data paths failed open. It is harmless as a display
   mapping, but the guard is right to be strict. It is now an explicit table, and no run has failed it since.
2. **The trail would have errored for a client manager** deep-linking `/requests` (not in their
   nav): the history endpoint is staff-only. The trail now renders for staff only.
3. **`useSearchParams` would have broken `next build`**: it needs a Suspense boundary on a
   statically prerendered page. `?r=` is read from `window.location` in the mount effect
   instead.

## Notes

  rule (no values) applies to requests too.
