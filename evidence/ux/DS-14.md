**Chip colours by kind:**
- procedures: statutory deadline, red tint;
- requests: due to a client, amber tint;
- tasks: internal, outlined;
- events: grey.

The prototype's Meeting / Interview / Portal appointment need a stored event type, so the
legend says they are coming soon.

**Days:** a deadline belongs to its UTC due date. A timed event belongs to the viewer's
**local** day, so an 01:00 Riyadh meeting is not filed under the previous UTC day.

## Bugs caught while verifying

1. **The phone dots were invisible.** They reused the chips' 10% tints, which are invisible at
   6px. They now use solid tones per kind (`KIND_DOT`).
2. **Arabic weekday names truncated** at 375 ("…بعاء"). The grid uses narrow weekday names
   below `sm`.
3. Prettier reformatted untouched parts of the calendar spec and controller, which would have
   buried the change. Those were restored, so the diff holds only the new test and the owner
   lines.

## Found, not fixed

- **The calendar treats a *rejected* procedure as finished** (CAL-02's terminal set), while the
  Work queue and the records count it as open: GRO's workflow goes rejected → in progress, a
  retry. So a rejected procedure with a due date is on the queue but not on the calendar.
  It predates this card and is filed as a follow-up.
- A `/today-preview` route has been committed since SS-06b, a leftover preview. It is to be
  reviewed with the Overview card.
