## Bugs caught while verifying

- **The two coming-soon page files were empty (0 bytes).** A zsh loop used `path` as its
  variable, and zsh ties `path` to `PATH`, so `cat` could not be found. The pages were
  recreated and the build now prerenders both. This is worth remembering for any shell loop.
- **The search's "Coming soon" marker cut the placeholder down to "Search people, refs, I".**
  It now says "Soon"/«قريبًا» with `pe-12`, and the placeholder shows in full.
- **Saved views first rendered below Other tools.** In the prototype it follows Workspace
  directly, and it now does.
