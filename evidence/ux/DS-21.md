## Owner decisions (with this card)

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| Who sees the Access table, and its source | **Everyone who reaches Settings, from a small read-only API**: `GET /access`, computed from the rules the API enforces |
| Where today's settings go | **Tabs: Access · Preferences · System**. Access first (the prototype's screen); Preferences for everyone; System for the Administrator only |

## Source

The prototype's Settings (`design/…dc.html` line 2711; data `matrix` near line 7193): a single "Access" table — field group × role, "What each role can see on an employee record." Its rows: employee profile and role · document expiry dates · government identifiers · pay and bank details · client companies · scheduling calendar.

## What changed

### API

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `employees/domain/employee-view.ts` | **One source for the visibility rules.** `staffVisibility(can)` (pay behind `salary.read`, government data in full behind `govdata.read`) and `PORTAL_EMPLOYEE_VISIBILITY` (status/expiry only) now live here. The employees controller **enforces** with `staffVisibility`, the portal with the constant. Before, both were written inline in their controllers |
| `employees/api/access.controller.ts` (new) | `GET /access`: six field groups × six roles, levels `full · own_company · own_record · hidden · none`. Rules per kind of role:<br>• **Staff:** their bundle through `staffVisibility`.<br>• **Client manager:** `PORTAL_EMPLOYEE_VISIBILITY`, limited to their own company.<br>• **Employee:** the self view (own record, with identifier numbers and pay, per ADR-011).<br>• **Client companies and calendar rows:** from `client.read`, `calendar.read` and `portal.read`.<br>Gated by `config.read-self`; `scopeOf()` refuses the employee principal |
| `@hr/contracts` `access.ts` (new) | `accessResponseSchema` (`roles`, `rows[{ group, access }]`), group and level enums |
| Isolation registry | `GET /access` → `client-read` (staff and client reps read it; anonymous 401; the employee fence 403) |

**`test/access-api.e2e-spec.ts` (new, 4 cases):**
1. The shape: roles, then six groups in order, each with all six roles.
2. The values the rules decide: profile; client manager identifiers **hidden** and expiries own company; pay hidden for the GRO officer and the client manager, full for the HR officer; the employee's own record; clients; calendar.
3. **It agrees with the real endpoints.** For a seed employee with an iqama number and a salary, `GET /employees/:id`:
   - as the **HR officer**: `salary` and `iqamaNumber` are present exactly when the table says Full;
   - as the **GRO officer**: `salary` is null (Hidden), and the iqama number and expiry are present (Full).
4. The employee → 403; anonymous → 401; the Auditor (MFA) reads it.

**Proven red first:** all 4 failed with `404` before the controller existed.

**Deviations from the prototype, kept on purpose:**
- **Six role columns, not four.** The prototype has no Auditor or employee column.
- **The client-companies row says Full / Own company, not "All six" / "Own only".** One vocabulary for the whole table.
- **Settings keeps its title, and the prototype's "Access" heading names the first tab and the table.** The other two tabs hold settings the prototype doesn't have.
