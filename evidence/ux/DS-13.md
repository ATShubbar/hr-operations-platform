## Owner decision (with this card)

**Keep `/tasks` as Task history.** It defaults to finished and cancelled tasks (the only place
they are listed). The queue and its dialog handle open work.

### The dialog

- **Facts:** Employee (the procedure's person, with the Arabic name) or "Raised by" (a
  request's requester); Client; Due (date, days chip, Hijri), or "No due date".
- **Detail:** a heading per kind ("Where this stands" / "What was asked for" / "What needs
  doing") with a kind badge; the text is the procedure's notes, the request's description or
  the task's description.
- **Procedures:** "Step *n* of 5" plus the current status. The bar is **derived from the
  real status** (not started → in progress → submitted → approved → completed); a rejected
  one sits at step 3 and is drawn red.
- **Tasks:** an editor for status and priority, with Save enabled only when something
  changed.
- **Owner:** the assignee "· owns this item", with **Open record** (the person, or the
  request).
- **Attachments and comments:** "coming soon".
- **Footer:** Snooze seven days (real date, DS-12 rule); Close; the primary action is the
  GRO status control, Mark done, or Open request.

## Bugs caught while verifying

1. **I reused a message key that was already in use.** `queue.kindLabel` ("Kind of work") was
   the pill group's accessible name, and the dialog's kind badge overwrote it with an object.
   next-intl logged `INSUFFICIENT_PATH`, and the pills lost their label. The badge now uses
   `queue.kindName`; the pills read "Kind of work" again (measured).
2. **The dialog overflowed sideways on a phone in Arabic** (351px of content in a 343px
   dialog). `DialogContent` is a grid whose items default to `min-width: auto`, so a
   truncating line (the Arabic role «مسؤول علاقات حكومية · المسؤول عن هذا العنصر») set a
   minimum wider than the screen. `[&>*]:min-w-0` lets the items shrink and truncate; it is
   now 0px in both languages.
