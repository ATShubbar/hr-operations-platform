## Source

The prototype's tables (22 of 33 header cells are `padding: 8px 16px; background:
neutral-100; 500 12px/16px; muted`; body rows 44–60px with a top rule, the commonest
56px for two-line cells and 48px for single-line), its KPI tiles (11/16 uppercase caption,
600 30/36 figure at -0.02em, 12/16 sub-line, ring card radius 14, `ring-strong` on hover),
and its soft badges.

## Owner decision with this card

**Cell text 13px in English, 14px in Arabic** (recommended, approved). The prototype's
13px applied literally would make the default locale the harder one to read: Arabic
glyphs sit visibly smaller than Latin at the same body size.

## Correction to DS-02

DS-02 recorded that letter-spacing "pulls Arabic letters apart and breaks their joins".
Measured in this card: **Chrome 152 does not apply letter-spacing to Arabic at all** —
`الموظفون` at 40px is **147.92px** at `0`, `0.025em` and `0.2em`. The LTR-only guard stays
(harmless, and engines differ), but its stated reason was wrong. Corrected in
`app-nav.tsx`, `evidence/ux/DS-02.md`, BACKLOG and CLAUDE.md.
