**Definitions used:**
- Headcount and Saudi share exclude leavers.
- Open items = active procedures + open or in-progress requests and tasks.
- Late = the due date before today.
- Workload counts the assignee; unassigned work is its own line.
- Service level = requests still `open` (awaiting a decision) with a due date, inside versus
  past it. Undated ones are counted separately and said to be.

## Found, not fixed

- **The detailed Workforce report defines headcount differently.** It shows Clients 5,
  Headcount 39, Saudization 53.85%, against the dashboard's 4 active clients, 36 and 53%.
  REP-01 counts every client, including archived ones, and every employee, including
  terminated ones. The dashboard, People and the client records exclude both. Both are right
  by their own definition, but two headcounts on one page will confuse. It is filed as a
  follow-up: align REP-01's definition, or label each.
